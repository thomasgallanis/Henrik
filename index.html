01 / Perspective

A career shaped
by a wider view.

Mike’s path spans Germany, Canada and the United Kingdom. That international background informs how he approaches global markets: as connected systems, understood through context rather than in isolation.

Raised in Canada after being born in Germany, Mike developed an early interest in business, economics, technology and the forces that move markets. What began as curiosity grew into academic study and more than 15 years of market involvement, according to his profile.

Today, he is professionally based in London. From there, he follows international developments, conducts research and works with a client network described as spanning more than 15 countries.

02 / Method

Process before impulse.

Active trading calls for attention to what is happening—and discipline about what could happen next. Mike describes his approach as structured, multi-factor analysis, with risk considered throughout.

01 / Observe

Read the conditions

Market structure, price action, volume, volatility and liquidity help frame the environment.

02 / Context

Connect the signals

Technical readings sit alongside economic developments, sentiment and potential catalysts.

03 / Plan

Define the risk

Position sizing, exposure and entry and exit planning are part of considering a trade.

04 / Adapt

Stay with the process

When conditions shift, reassessment matters more than reacting emotionally to each move.

05 / Learn

Keep refining

Research and continued learning are central in markets that never stand still.
